The cost of leaving it

The longer it waits, the bigger the job.

Heating and plumbing problems rarely stay small. This is how it usually goes.

  1. 01

    An old boiler burns more oil

    Boilers get less efficient as they age, and older models were never as efficient as the ones made today. You pay for that every time the tank is filled, year after year.

  2. 02

    A small leak doesn't stay small

    A drip under the sink or behind the bath soaks into floorboards, joists and ceilings. By the time a stain shows up, it has usually been going on for a while.

  3. 03

    Boilers pick the coldest week

    That's when they work hardest, so that's when worn parts give up. And it's the same week everyone else is ringing for help.

A planned job happens on a day that suits you. A breakdown happens whenever it likes.

No heat at all

Most likely cause
Often something simple: the oil has run out, the timer or thermostat has been knocked, or the burner has locked out. It can also be a failed pump or a fault on the burner itself.
What we'd do
Run through the basics with you over the phone first. If it's not something you can sort yourself, we'll come out, find the fault and fix it, or tell you straight if the boiler is past it.
Worth checking first
Check the oil gauge on the tank, the timer and the thermostat. If the reset button on the burner is lit, press it once only. If it locks out again, leave it and ring us.
Get This Sorted More on Central Heating

Safety first. If you smell oil fumes, turn the boiler off and open windows and doors to ventilate. If you smell gas, don't touch light switches or use a naked flame. Leave the house and call Gas Networks Ireland on 1800 20 50 50.

SEAI grants

Grants can help. Here's the plain version.

The SEAI (Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland) runs grant schemes for home energy upgrades. Grants may be available for heat pumps and some heating upgrades.

Check the latest at seai.ie Ask Us on a Free Visit
  1. 1

    Grants may be available

    For heat pumps and some heating upgrades, depending on the scheme and your home.

  2. 2

    The rules change

    Who qualifies, what's covered and how much you get all change from time to time. That's why we don't put amounts here. Check seai.ie for the current figures.

  3. 3

    Approval usually comes first

    With most SEAI grants you need approval before any work starts, or you could lose the grant. Check before you commit.
